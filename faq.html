Frequently Asked Questions

If your question isn't answered here or on The Races page, email race@jumbo-holdsworth.co.nz.

General

When and where is the race?

Saturday 30th January 2027 (Auckland Anniversary weekend) at Mt Holdsworth in the Tararua Forest Park, near Masterton.

What race options are there?

  • Jumbo→Holdsworth (anti-clockwise) — 24km, 1500m elevation. 2027 podium direction.
  • Holdsworth→Jumbo (clockwise) — 24km, 1500m elevation.
  • Hooper Loop — 12km, 500m elevation, mostly in the bush.

What's the difference between the two directions?

Jumbo→Holdsworth has a flat start followed by a steep, technical climb. Holdsworth→Jumbo has a more graded climb at the start, then a technical descent and a flat, fast finish along the river. For 2027, the podium direction is Jumbo first.

How hard is the course?

The 24km course is challenging, with 1500m of climbing and exposed ridge sections. It needs good fitness and mountain/trail experience — most people take about as long as they would to run a marathon. The Hooper Loop is less demanding, with 500m of climbing mostly within the bush.

Entry

How much does it cost?

$88 for either direction of the 24km race and $65 for the 12km Hooper Loop, plus a $5 admin and credit card fee. Entries include a custom wool-blend race beanie (it counts as the wool hat on the mandatory gear list) and a post-race lunch. Beanies are handed out at registration, so you need to be at the event to collect yours. See Entry.

Is there a limit on numbers?

Yes — 130 per direction on the main course and 300 across all races. Entries have sold out about a month before closing for the last three years.

What if I need to withdraw or transfer?

Withdrawals before 1 January 2027 get a 50% refund; no refunds after that. Transfers to another person are possible until 12pm 18 January 2027 for a $20 admin fee.

Are there age limits?

24km: over 18 as at race day (30 January 2027). Hooper Loop: over 14. Younger runners may be accepted at the organisers' discretion — email race@jumbo-holdsworth.co.nz.

Race Day

What are the start times?

24km: registration 6:30am, briefing 7:45am, start 8:00am.
Hooper Loop: registration 7:45am, briefing 8:45am, start 9:00am.

What mandatory gear do I need?

See the full list on The Races page. Gear is checked at registration — no exceptions.

Are there cut-off times?

Yes, and they're strict. Jumbo→Holdsworth: 1hr 10min to Atiwhakatu Hut and 2hr 20min to Jumbo Hut. Holdsworth→Jumbo: 1hr 50min to Powell Hut. If you don't think you'll make them, enter the Hooper Loop.

Are there aid stations?

Marshals with radios at Jumbo, Powell and Atiwhakatu huts record splits; the huts have water tanks and lollies. There are also marshals on the summits of Jumbo and Holdsworth. Water availability depends on the season and is confirmed at briefing.

What if I pull out during the race?

You MUST report to the race director or timing tent at race control before leaving the park so we can account for you. This is crucial for safety.

What are the race categories?

Junior (under 21), Senior (21–39), Vet (40–49) and Super Vet (50+), each with women's and men's divisions — plus the Powell Hut Sub 60/Sub 70 clubs, King and Queen of the Mountain, and Midpacker prizes. Details.

Is there accommodation?

The race starts and finishes at the DOC Mt Holdsworth campsite, where camping is available (booking required, DOC fees apply). DOC website.
