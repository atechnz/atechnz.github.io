2027 Entry

Entries for the 33rd Jumbo-Holdsworth Trail Race — Saturday 30th January 2027 — are open. The race has sold out about a month before entries close for the last three years, so don't leave it late.

Register Here →

Entry Fees

Jumbo→Holdsworth / Holdsworth→Jumbo$88
Hooper Loop$65

Plus a $5 admin and credit card fee per online entry. Entries include a custom wool-blend race beanie and a post-race lunch.

Entry Terms & Conditions

  • Entries are limited to 130 competitors in each direction on the main course, and 300 competitors in total on the day — whichever fills first.
  • There is a waitlist for each race if you miss out, including the option to change direction if one sells out.
  • Entries close at 12pm Monday 18th January 2027.
  • Until 12pm 18th January 2027 you can downgrade to the Hooper Loop, or swap direction on the main course, at no charge — use the link in your confirmation email.
  • Until 12pm 18th January 2027 you can transfer your entry to another person for a $20 admin fee. After that, if you can no longer run you will need to withdraw.
  • No course changes or transfers after 12pm 18th January 2027.
  • 24km competitors must be over 18 as at 30/01/2027 and Hooper Loop competitors over 14. Younger runners may be able to race at the organisers' discretion — email race@jumbo-holdsworth.co.nz.

Refunds

  • Withdrawals before 1st January 2027 receive a 50% refund.
  • Withdrawals from 1st January 2027 onwards receive no refund.
